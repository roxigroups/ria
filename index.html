What RIA is

RIA is not another chatbot.

RIA is being built as an Agentic AI workforce for distribution businesses — AI that can understand a business objective, interact with people, make decisions within defined rules and trigger the next action.

Traditional automation

A script that runs.

Rule→ Trigger→ Action

The system does exactly what it was told, in the order it was told. Anything outside the script falls back to a person — usually after the fact.

Agentic RIA

An agent that works towards an objective.

Objective→ Understand→ Decide→ Act→ Learn from outcome→ Follow up

RIA is being designed to hold a real conversation, read the response, decide the next step inside rules you define, and leave behind structured information your team can act on.

Collection Agent workflow

From overdue to action.

Eight steps, in order. Each one is being built as a defined stage the agent moves through — not a black box.

01

Know

RIA receives the relevant customer and account information — outstanding amount, ageing, contact, and the history your team already maintains.

02

Prioritise

The agent identifies which accounts need attention today, based on the business rules you configure — ageing, value, promise date, or route.

03

Call

RIA initiates the collection conversation and identifies itself clearly as an AI agent calling on behalf of your business.

04

Understand

The agent interprets what the customer actually said — agreement, delay, dispute, a request to call back — rather than matching a fixed keyword.

05

Capture

The useful part of the conversation becomes fields.

  • Payment commitment
  • Expected date
  • Reason for delay
  • Dispute indication
  • Follow-up request
  • Escalation requirement
06

Follow up

RIA can schedule or trigger the next defined follow-up, so a promise made on a call does not depend on someone remembering it.

07

Intelligence

The conversation becomes structured business intelligence — visible across accounts, routes and payment cycles instead of sitting inside one person's notebook.

08

Escalate

Exceptions are routed to a human team member with the summary, the commitment and the reason already attached.
